# Luffy look mechanics

The straw-hat, hair, torso, feet, and baseline stay anchored.  Luffy's pupils and face lead the gaze; the head turns or tilts slightly toward the target and the hat brim follows by a small matching shift.  The body must never be rotated as a whole.

- **000 up:** pupils high under the brim; chin lifted slightly.
- **090 screen-right:** nose, pupils, and hat tilt visibly toward the viewer's right; right side of face is more visible.
- **180 down:** pupils low; brim dips slightly over the eyes.
- **270 screen-left:** mirror of screen-right in viewer coordinates; left side of face is more visible.
- **Diagonals:** advance in even 22.5-degree steps between the cardinal poses while preserving the same small chibi scale and foot baseline.

No new props, text, scenery, shadows, detached effects, motion lines, or whole-sprite rotation.  Keep the existing crisp pixel-art outline, warm palette, red vest, blue shorts, straw hat, and compact body proportions.
